Technical Architecture: How It Works

Ultra Age AI v6.0 runs on a high-precision Client-Side JavaScript Engine with several critical upgrades over standard calculators.

1. Dirty-Checking RAF Loop

The engine uses a requestAnimationFrame loop with state-comparison (dirty checking). The DOM is only repainted when a value has actually changed, eliminating unnecessary renders and dramatically reducing CPU load and battery drain on mobile devices.

2. Age-Tiered Biological Algorithms

Consumption and biological values (heartbeats, food, water, steps) are calculated using age-bracket multipliers. A newborn's caloric intake and heart rate differ substantially from an adult's — the engine accounts for this with segmented day-bucket calculations across each life phase.

3. Exact Gregorian Leap-Year Accounting

Rather than using fractional year constants, the engine performs a day-by-day calendar traversal (bucketed by year) to count exact leap years lived, then cross-references Date.UTC to eliminate timezone drift errors.

Intergalactic Distance: The Physics

You are always moving through space, even sitting still. Three velocities are combined:

  • Earth's Orbit (Solar): Earth travels ~107,226 km/h around the Sun.
  • Solar System (Galactic): Our entire solar system orbits the Milky Way's core at ~828,000 km/h (NASA data).
  • Milky Way (Universal): The galaxy itself moves through the observable universe at approximately 2,160,000 km/h relative to the CMB.

Multiplying these speeds by your total hours alive gives a genuinely staggering number — a visceral reminder of cosmic motion.

Generational Cohorts Explained

Your generation is determined by your birth year using the widely-accepted Pew Research and Beresford Research cohort definitions: Silent Generation (1928–1945), Baby Boomers (1946–1964), Gen X (1965–1980), Millennials (1981–1996), Gen Z (1997–2012), and Gen Alpha (2013–present).

Biological & Vital Algorithms

The Bio-Engine uses age-segmented heart rate and breath rate baselines based on published medical literature:

  • Infants (0–1 yr): ~140 bpm / 44 breaths/min
  • Children (1–10 yr): ~95 bpm / 26 breaths/min
  • Adolescents (10–18 yr): ~80 bpm / 20 breaths/min
  • Adults (18–60 yr): ~72 bpm / 16 breaths/min
  • Seniors (60+ yr): ~68 bpm / 14 breaths/min
