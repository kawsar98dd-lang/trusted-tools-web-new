What is the difference between Base-2 and Base-10 storage calculations?

In Base-10 (Decimal), 1 KB = 1000 bytes — used by hard drive manufacturers and network speeds. In Base-2 (Binary), 1 KiB = 1024 bytes — used by operating systems. OmniConvert Ultra lets you toggle between both standards in the Storage category.

Can I convert compound units like 5 feet 8 inches?

Yes. OmniConvert Ultra supports compound unit inputs for Length (e.g., 5 ft 8 in) and Weight (e.g., 6 lbs 4 oz). Simply tap the compound icon in the From box when using Length or Weight categories.
