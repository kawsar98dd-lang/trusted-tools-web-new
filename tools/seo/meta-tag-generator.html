Advanced SEO Knowledge Base

The "Fold" & Descriptions

Meta descriptions don't directly impact rankings, but they determine Click-Through Rate (CTR). A strong CTR signals relevance to Google. Place your primary keyword early and treat the description as ad copy to win the click.

Optimizing for Slack & Discord

Teams share links via chat apps daily. These platforms rely on og:image and twitter:card. Our tool ensures your link "unfurls" correctly with a large image, making your content look professional and trustworthy in private channels.

Punctuation & Pixel Width

Google truncates titles by pixel width, not character count. A "W" takes more space than an "i". Using pipes (|) instead of hyphens (-) saves pixels. Our tool calculates this exact pixel width using the Arial font metric used in SERPs.

Mobile & Dark Mode

The theme-color meta tag changes the browser address bar color on mobile devices. By matching your site's header color you provide a native app-like experience that increases user retention and reduces bounce rates.

Why doesn't my Facebook image show?

Likely a missing og:image tag or wrong dimensions. Facebook recommends 1200×630 px. If your image is too small, Facebook might ignore it or display a small thumbnail instead.
