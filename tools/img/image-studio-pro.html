Ultra Image Titanium: The Architecture of Privacy

In an era where data sovereignty is paramount, Ultra Image Titanium redefines image processing. Unlike traditional SaaS tools that offload processing to remote cloud servers, Titanium operates as a Client-Side Wasm Application.

This means the compression engine (Algorithm: Titanium-Quant) is downloaded once to your browser's temporary memory. From that moment on, every pixel is manipulated locally on your CPU/GPU. Your sensitive photos, contracts, or IDs never leave your device. This architecture ensures Zero-Knowledge Privacy and enterprise-grade security compliance.

Advanced Workflow Guide

1

Multi-Threaded Ingestion

Drag & drop up to 50 files. Our engine utilizes Web Workers to parse JPG, PNG, WEBP, and even Apple's High-Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) concurrently without freezing your UI.

2

Binary Search Optimization

Define a hard limit (e.g., "50 KB"). The core engine performs a binary search iteration, compressing and re-measuring the blob until it hits your exact target size with the highest possible visual fidelity.

3

Secure Metadata Scrubbing

Toggle "Scrub GPS/EXIF" to automatically strip geolocation tags, camera models, and shutter settings — crucial for photographers sharing work publicly.

4

Smart Export

Download optimized files individually, as a bulk ZIP archive, or compile them into a PDF document. The PDF engine automatically detects landscape vs. portrait orientation for a professional layout.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does the HEIC conversion work?

We utilize a Wasm-compiled decoder (heic2any) that runs directly in your browser tab. It converts the HEIC buffer to a raw JPEG blob before our compression engine takes over. No data is sent to a backend.

Q: Why is there no "Upload" progress bar?

Because there is no upload! Files are read from your disk into the browser's allocated RAM instantly. The progress bar you see is for CPU Processing, not data transfer.
