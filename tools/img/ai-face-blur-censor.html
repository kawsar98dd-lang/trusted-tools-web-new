How to Use AI Privacy Censor ULTRA MAX

  • Step 1: Upload Your Image
    Drag and drop any JPG, PNG, WEBP, or BMP image into the upload zone, or click to browse your files. Your image never leaves your device.
  • Step 2: Choose Censor Modes
    Enable AI Face Censor to auto-detect faces, OCR Text Redact to find emails/phones/cards, and Magic Brush for manual painting. Mix and match as needed.
  • Step 3: Run AI Censor
    Click "Run AI Censor" to trigger the MediaPipe face model and Tesseract OCR engine. Results appear live on the canvas.
  • Step 4: Refine with Magic Brush
    Enable the Magic Brush and paint over any remaining areas you wish to censor. Use the Undo button to reverse strokes.
  • Step 5: Download Clean Image
    Hit "Download Image" to export a privacy-cleaned PNG with all EXIF metadata stripped. Your original file is untouched.

Technology Stack: How It Works

This tool uses a cutting-edge stack of browser-native AI libraries to process everything locally:

  • MediaPipe Tasks Vision (Face Detector): Google's production-grade ML model runs entirely via WebAssembly in your browser, detecting face bounding boxes with sub-100ms latency.
  • Tesseract.js (OCR Engine): A WebAssembly port of the industry-standard Tesseract 4 OCR engine. It reads image text and our regex engine flags sensitive patterns (emails, phone numbers, 16-digit card numbers).
  • HTML5 Canvas API: All blur, pixelation, and black-bar effects are rendered via raw Canvas 2D context operations — no server-side image processing whatsoever.
  • JSZip + FileSaver.js: Batch output is packaged into a ZIP archive entirely in memory and triggered as a browser download with zero server involvement.
  • EXIF Stripping: By re-drawing source images onto a fresh Canvas and exporting as PNG blob, all EXIF metadata (GPS, camera model, timestamps) is inherently discarded.

Privacy & Security Guarantee

This tool operates 100% Client-Side. Every byte of AI processing happens within your browser's sandboxed environment:

  • Your images are never uploaded to any server.
  • No image data, metadata, or OCR results are ever logged or transmitted.
  • AI models are loaded from public CDNs once and cached by your browser.
  • Works fully offline after the first load (models cached via browser storage).
  • EXIF stripping ensures exported images contain zero geolocation or device fingerprint data.
