Advanced AI & Privacy Knowledge Base

How the AI Works

The tool uses an ONNX Runtime + WebAssembly-based segmentation model from @imgly/background-removal. The model runs entirely inside your browser tab using a Web Worker, preventing UI freezes while handling large images with high fidelity.

Zero Upload, Zero Risk

Traditional background removers like Remove.bg send your files to remote servers. Our tool processes everything locally — your images are never transmitted anywhere, making it ideal for confidential product photos, ID documents, and personal portraits.

Stroke & Glow Technique

The stroke effect is generated via a multi-pass canvas algorithm: the alpha mask is dilated by n pixels in all directions to create the stroke region, then filled with your chosen color beneath the subject layer. This produces crisp, non-destructive outlines at any width.

Local History with IndexedDB

The last 5 processed images are stored in your browser's IndexedDB database — a structured, offline-capable storage built into every modern browser. This is far more robust than localStorage, supporting blobs up to hundreds of MB without any server dependency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this background remover really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No account needed, no watermarks, no daily limits. The AI model runs entirely in your browser for free.

Does it work offline?

After the AI model is downloaded once (approx. 50MB, cached by your browser), the tool functions fully offline. No internet connection is required to process images.

What image types and sizes are supported?

Supports JPG, PNG, and WebP. For best AI accuracy and performance, images up to 4000×4000px are recommended. Very large files (25MB+) may require a few extra seconds depending on your device.

Why does the first image take longer to process?

On the very first run, the browser downloads and caches the ONNX segmentation model (~50MB). Subsequent images process immediately from cache — even after closing and reopening the page.

Is my data safe and private?

Absolutely. All processing uses client-side JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your images never leave your device. The IndexedDB history is also stored only in your local browser and is never synced or transmitted.
