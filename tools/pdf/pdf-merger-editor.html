How It Works (God Mode)

Unlike standard PDF tools that upload your sensitive documents to a server, Ultra PDF AI uses WebAssembly technology (pdf-lib & pdf.js) to process everything directly inside your browser.

  • Visual Sorting: Pages render as thumbnails. Drag them to change their order before merging.
  • Hybrid Merging: Mix PDF pages and image files (JPG/PNG) in the same export document.
  • Blank Pages: Insert blank A4 divider pages anywhere in the document structure.
  • Zero Latency: No server round-trip. Processing is instant and entirely local.
  • Privacy First: Your files never leave your device — perfect for confidential documents.
