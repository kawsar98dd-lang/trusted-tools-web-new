Everyday Utilities: 5 Free Online Tools

This group collects practical utilities that do not fit a single technical category: building a resume, staying focused with a Pomodoro timer, creating avatars, generating Gmail alias variants and producing fictional names and profiles.

They are lightweight and run in your browser, so they are quick to open and need no account.

Who it is for: Job seekers, students, remote workers, testers and writers.

Tools in this category

  • Resume & CV Builder

    Build a resume with live preview, drag-and-drop sections and a keyword matcher for job descriptions, then export it as PDF. Your data stays in the browser.

    Good for: job seekers, students.

  • Avatar Generator

    Create unique avatars from any seed in styles like 8-bit retro, neural circuit and cyber glitch, and download them as images.

    Good for: gamers, developers, designers.

  • Pomodoro Focus Timer

    A Pomodoro timer with focus and break cycles, optional pink noise and binaural beats, and simple session tracking.

    Good for: students, remote workers.

  • Gmail Alias Generator (Dot & Plus Trick)

    Generate many Gmail address variants using the dot and plus tricks. All variants deliver to the same inbox, which is handy for filters and sign-up tracking.

    Good for: privacy-minded users, testers.

  • Random Name & User Profile Generator

    Generate random names, emails and profile data for many regions and export JSON or TXT for testing, mock-ups and writing. Data is fictional.

    Good for: developers, writers, testers.

Privacy, processing and limits

  • The Resume Builder keeps your resume data in your browser; export your PDF before clearing site data. The name and profile generator produces fictional data only.
  • Gmail aliases made with dots or plus signs all deliver to the same inbox. Some websites reject plus addresses, so test with the service you need.

See the privacy policy for how the site itself handles data.
