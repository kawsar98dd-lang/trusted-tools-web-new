PDF Tools: 4 Free Online Tools

The PDF tools cover the most common paperwork tasks: combining files, shrinking a PDF for email, turning photos into a PDF, signing a document and extracting text with OCR.

Because PDFs often contain personal or business information, these tools process files in your browser instead of uploading them to a conversion server.

Who it is for: Students, job applicants, office workers and freelancers who handle forms and scanned documents.

Tools in this category

  • Image to PDF Converter

    Combine JPG, PNG or HEIC images into one PDF. Reorder pages, rotate, set page size and quality, all in your browser without uploading files.

    Good for: students, office workers.

  • PDF Compressor

    Reduce the size of a PDF for email or upload forms. Compression runs in your browser and the file is not sent to a server. Savings vary by file.

    Good for: job applicants, students.

  • Merge, Reorder & Edit PDF Pages

    Combine several PDFs into one, reorder, rotate and delete pages and mix in images. Everything is done in your browser, so files stay on your device.

    Good for: office workers, students.

  • Sign PDF and Extract Text with OCR

    Draw a signature on a PDF, run Tesseract OCR to extract text, and add a SHA-256 fingerprint to check the file has not changed. Runs in your browser.

    Good for: freelancers, office workers.

Privacy, processing and limits

  • Processing runs on your device, so very large files may be slow on older phones. Compression savings vary: scanned or image-heavy PDFs shrink more than text-only ones.
  • The PDF signing tool draws a signature image and a file fingerprint; it is not a certificate-based legal e-signature service.

See the privacy policy for how the site itself handles data.
