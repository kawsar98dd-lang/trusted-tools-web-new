Text Tools: 9 Free Online Tools

The text tools help you count, compare, clean, extract and restyle text: a word counter with reading time, a side-by-side diff checker, a developer-oriented text editor, a bulk extractor for emails, phone numbers and URLs, and playful converters like fancy fonts and text to handwriting.

Pasted text is processed in your browser, which is useful for drafts, code and contact lists you would rather not paste into a server-based tool.

Who it is for: Writers, students, developers, marketers and support teams.

Tools in this category

  • Online Text Editor with Developer Utilities

    A text workspace for formatting JSON and SQL, testing regular expressions, generating UUIDs and SHA-256 hashes, and other text transformations.

    Good for: developers, writers.

  • Email, Phone & URL Extractor

    Pull emails, phone numbers, URLs, IP addresses or your own regex matches out of pasted text, files or images (OCR). Works in your browser.

    Good for: marketers, analysts, developers.

  • Diff Checker: Compare Text & Code

    Paste two versions of text, code, JSON or HTML and see added, removed and changed lines side by side. The comparison happens in your browser.

    Good for: developers, writers, editors.

  • Fancy Font & Text Style Generator

    Turn plain text into fancy Unicode fonts, Zalgo glitch text, ASCII art or Morse code to paste into bios and posts. Some apps may not display every style.

    Good for: social media users.

  • Text to Handwriting Converter

    Turn typed text into handwriting-style pages in English or Bangla, adjust ink and paper, and export a PDF or images.

    Good for: students.

  • Bulk Phone Number Formatter

    Add a country code, prefix or suffix to a list of phone numbers and generate WhatsApp or Telegram links in bulk. Formatting only; no numbers are checked.

    Good for: marketers, support teams.

  • Text to Speech Studio

    Convert text, PDF files or OCR-read images to speech with your browser’s voices and download audio. Available voices depend on your device and browser.

    Good for: students, content creators.

  • Typing Speed Test for Coding (WPM)

    Practise typing real code snippets and measure net WPM and accuracy. Useful for programmers who want to type symbols and brackets faster.

    Good for: programmers, students.

  • Word Counter & Text Analyzer

    Count words, characters, sentences and reading time, check keyword density and compare your length to platform limits such as X (Twitter).

    Good for: writers, students, SEO beginners.

Privacy, processing and limits

  • Text to Speech uses voices provided by your browser and operating system, so available voices and quality differ by device.
  • The Bulk Data Extractor can read text from images using OCR that runs in your browser; extraction accuracy depends on image quality.

See the privacy policy for how the site itself handles data.
