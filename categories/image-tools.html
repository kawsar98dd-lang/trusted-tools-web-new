Image Tools: 11 Free Online Tools

The image tools cover three jobs: preparing pictures (compressing, converting HEIC, upscaling, vectorizing), protecting privacy (removing EXIF location data, blurring faces, redacting text) and creating graphics (QR codes, memes, device mockups, YouTube thumbnails).

Images are opened in your browser rather than uploaded to a tool server, which matters for photos that contain people, documents or location data.

Who it is for: Photographers, online sellers, bloggers, designers and anyone sharing screenshots or photos.

Tools in this category

  • 3D Device Mockup Generator

    Place a screenshot on a 3D iPhone or MacBook model, adjust the angle and lighting, and export a high-resolution PNG. Rendered with WebGL in your browser.

    Good for: designers, app makers, marketers.

  • Image Background Remover

    Remove the background from a photo, then add a solid colour, outline or drop shadow and export PNG or WebP. Batch and ZIP export supported.

    Good for: sellers, designers, creators.

  • Blur Faces & Redact Sensitive Text in Images

    Detect and blur faces, redact emails, phone numbers or card numbers found by OCR, or brush over areas by hand before you share a picture.

    Good for: people sharing screenshots, journalists.

  • Image Upscaler (4x / 8x)

    Enlarge a low-resolution image 4x or 8x with an in-browser AI model. Model files load from a CDN on first use; your picture is not uploaded.

    Good for: photographers, sellers, designers.

  • Document Scanner with OCR and PDF Export

    Scan paper documents with your phone or webcam, auto-crop edges, extract text with Tesseract OCR and export multi-page PDFs from your browser.

    Good for: students, office workers.

  • EXIF Metadata Remover

    View and remove EXIF data such as GPS location, camera model and timestamps from your photos before sharing. Processed locally in your browser.

    Good for: privacy-conscious photo sharers.

  • Image Compressor & HEIC Converter

    Compress JPG, PNG and WebP, convert HEIC photos and resize images in batches. Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.

    Good for: bloggers, sellers, iPhone users.

  • Meme Generator

    Make memes with a multi-layer editor, built-in templates or your own image, and export PNG or JPG without a watermark.

    Good for: social media users, creators.

  • QR Code Generator

    Create QR codes for links, WiFi, contacts (vCard), WhatsApp, crypto and more, with custom colours and an optional logo. Download as PNG or SVG.

    Good for: business owners, event organisers.

  • PNG & JPG to SVG Converter

    Trace PNG or JPG images into SVG vectors and export SVG, React JSX or Flutter code. Conversion runs in your browser.

    Good for: designers, front-end developers.

  • YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

    Paste a YouTube link to download its thumbnail in available sizes, then adjust brightness, contrast or borders in a small editor. Only for videos you may use.

    Good for: content creators, designers.

Privacy, processing and limits

  • The Image Upscaler loads its AI model files from a CDN the first time you use it. The YouTube Thumbnail Downloader contacts YouTube’s image servers and a metadata and image-proxy service to fetch the picture you ask for.
  • The Background Remover needs AI model files that are not installed on this site yet, so it currently shows an error instead of removing backgrounds.

See the privacy policy for how the site itself handles data.
