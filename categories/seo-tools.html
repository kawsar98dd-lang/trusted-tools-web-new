SEO & Webmaster Tools: 6 Free Online Tools

These tools generate the small files and snippets that help a website be crawled and shared properly: meta tags and social cards, an XML sitemap, a robots.txt, favicons, plus hashtag ideas and a link shortener for promotion.

They create text and files for you to review and publish. They do not guarantee rankings; good content and a clear site structure matter more.

Who it is for: Site owners, bloggers, webmasters, marketers and developers launching a new website.

Tools in this category

  • Favicon Generator

    Turn an image into favicon.ico and PNG icon sizes plus a web manifest. Created in your browser and ready to download.

    Good for: site owners, developers.

  • Hashtag & Keyword Generator

    Get hashtag and keyword ideas for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook posts from your topic. Suggestions are ideas, not ranking guarantees.

    Good for: social media creators.

  • URL Shortener, UTM Builder & QR Code

    Shorten links through third-party services (is.gd, TinyURL), add UTM parameters and create a QR code. Links you shorten are sent to those services.

    Good for: marketers, social media users.

  • Meta Tag Generator with SERP Preview

    Write title tags, meta descriptions, Open Graph and Twitter Card tags with a live Google-style preview and length checks, then copy the HTML.

    Good for: site owners, SEO beginners.

  • Robots.txt Generator

    Build a robots.txt with allow and disallow rules, crawl-delay, a sitemap line and optional AI-crawler rules such as GPTBot. It is not access control.

    Good for: webmasters, site owners.

  • XML Sitemap Generator from a URL List

    Paste a list of URLs and generate a valid XML sitemap with priority, change frequency and last-modified values. It does not crawl your site.

    Good for: webmasters, SEO beginners.

Privacy, processing and limits

  • Meta tag, sitemap, robots.txt and favicon tools run in your browser. The Sitemap Generator builds a file from a URL list you provide; it does not crawl your site.
  • The URL Shortener sends the links you shorten to third-party services (such as is.gd and TinyURL), so do not shorten private links.

See the privacy policy for how the site itself handles data.
