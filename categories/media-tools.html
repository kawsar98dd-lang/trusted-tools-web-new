Audio & Video Tools: 4 Free Online Tools

These tools handle sound and video in the browser: trimming and enhancing audio, compressing or trimming video, recording your screen, and generating test tones.

Media files can be large, so speed depends on your device. Short clips work best on phones; longer recordings are better on a desktop browser.

Who it is for: Podcasters, teachers, content creators, students and people testing speakers or headphones.

Tools in this category

  • Online Audio Editor and Effects

    Trim audio, apply EQ, bass boost, voice effects and 8D panning, then export MP3 or WAV. Editing happens in your browser.

    Good for: podcasters, musicians, students.

  • Online Screen Recorder

    Record your screen, window or tab with webcam overlay and audio, draw while recording and save the video. No watermark. Uses your browser’s capture API.

    Good for: teachers, support teams, gamers.

  • Online Tone & Frequency Generator

    Play sine, square, sawtooth and triangle tones from 1 Hz to 22 kHz, sweeps and binaural beats, plus a speaker water-eject tone. Use at low volume.

    Good for: audio hobbyists, phone owners.

  • Video Compressor, Trimmer & GIF Maker

    Compress MP4, trim clips, make GIFs and extract audio using FFmpeg WebAssembly. Videos are processed locally; the FFmpeg engine loads on first use.

    Good for: creators, students, social media users.

Privacy, processing and limits

  • Editing and recording happen locally. The Video Studio loads its FFmpeg WebAssembly engine from a CDN the first time you use it, and the Screen Recorder asks your browser for permission to capture your screen, camera or microphone.
  • Play tones at a low volume first; high or very low frequencies can be unpleasant or harmful to speakers and hearing.

See the privacy policy for how the site itself handles data.
