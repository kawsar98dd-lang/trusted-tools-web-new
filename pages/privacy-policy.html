Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

At Trusted Tools Web, accessible from https://trustedtoolsweb.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Trusted Tools Web and how we use it.

Core Philosophy: Unlike other websites, our tools are built with "Client-Side" technology. This means your files, images, and texts never leave your device. The processing happens locally in your browser.

1. Information We Collect

Since we do not require user registration, we do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) like your name, address, or phone number directly.

However, like any other website hosting service, standard log files are generated. These files verify site integrity and may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamp
  • Referring/exit pages

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Trusted Tools Web uses "cookies" to store information about visitors' preferences (such as Dark/Light mode settings). We also use third-party cookies for advertising purposes (Google AdSense) to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our website and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. How We Handle Your Files

For tools like Image Compressor, PDF Converter, or Code Diff Checker:

  • Zero Upload Policy: We do not upload your files to any cloud server.
  • Local Processing: All calculations and conversions occur within your browser's memory using JavaScript.
  • Automatic Deletion: Since data is never stored, simply refreshing the page wipes all temporary data instantly.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, among other rights, California consumers have the right to request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the right to access, rectification, erasure, restrict processing, object to processing, and data portability.
